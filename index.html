About

Technology in service of learning

Stephen Wong is a technology leader in the education space with nearly two decades of experience. He has a proven track record of driving innovation and implementing cutting-edge solutions in educational environments, and his expertise in cloud computing, cybersecurity, and data analytics has been instrumental in transforming digital infrastructure for research and learning.

A native Californian and lifelong technophile, Stephen believes in the deliberate use of technology to spread ideas, build communities, and solve problems. He is passionate about creating inclusive and effective educational experiences for all learners — with a particular focus on access to learning, tools for student success, and diversity and inclusion. He also puts that belief into practice by building learning apps and games at Spaikz.

What I Do

Focus areas

Stephen works where technology, research, and learning meet. These are the areas he champions — and the ones he’s always happy to talk about.

Responsible AI

Helping faculty and staff adopt AI quickly and safely — with clear guidance, approved tools, and review paths that keep pace with innovation instead of slowing it down.

Research Data Governance

Protecting the data behind world-class education research with secure environments and clear, researcher-friendly processes.

Data & Analytics

Turning scattered systems into a single source of truth, so leaders can make decisions from clear, real-time insight.

Cloud & Infrastructure

Modernizing legacy systems into well-governed cloud platforms that are secure, resilient, and cost-effective.

Security & Privacy

Keeping people and information safe through thoughtful security practices, strong data protection, and a culture of awareness.

Digital Accessibility

Making digital experiences usable by everyone, and building accessibility in from the start rather than bolting it on later.

Approach

How Stephen leads

01

Enable, don’t block

Good governance should help people move faster. Stephen builds clear, quick paths for adopting new tools — including AI — so innovation and safety go hand in hand.

02

Systems over one-offs

He turns case-by-case handling into documented, repeatable processes that scale with the organization and outlast any single person.

03

Modernize with purpose

Every upgrade should leave things more secure, more accessible, and more sustainable than before — not just newer.

04

Lead with data

Decisions are better when everyone can see the same picture. Stephen connects data across systems so leaders get clear, honest answers.
